# CANARY - SOB-92 Clara Design

## Canary Plan
1. **Initial Deployment:** Deploy the Clara Design artifacts to the staging environment.
2. **Review:** Have the design team and UI/UX stakeholders review the artifacts against the original Bill research to ensure 100% fidelity.
3. **Pilot:** Implement the new design system in a non-critical Coach OS dashboard (e.g., Playground or Wind Direction).
4. **Monitoring:** Monitor for any visual regressions or missing design tokens.
5. **Full Rollout:** Once approved, roll out the design guidelines across all remaining Coach OS interfaces.
