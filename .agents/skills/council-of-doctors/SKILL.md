# Council of Doctors
1. Read campaign + durable evidence and request Graham recall when prior state can affect the decision.
2. Clara compiles a Mission Topology when work needs more than one bounded PDR: phase × orchestration pattern × capability cells × evidence/feedback loops.
3. Rick invokes Doctor13/11/12 only where the topology needs hierarchy; a mission may also use Pipeline, Swarm, Mesh, Orchestrator-Worker or Deterministic Reflex cells.
4. Doctors/Companions execute independent cells concurrently. Capabilities are reentrant and may appear multiple times in one mission.
5. Bounded reversible Build cells may start while unrelated Discover/Design cells continue. Never use role order as a completion gate.
6. Yaz/Clara/Graham may verify behavior/design/state in parallel; Nardole routes dependencies and return-to throughout the mission.
7. Monitor/Learn may reopen the smallest affected earlier cell without restarting the Council.
8. Use send_message / Discussion #185 before escalating unresolved cross-Core conflicts to Rick.
9. Rick selects the next cells from acceptance gaps and evidence, not ticket count.
10. Repeat until mission outcome and detachment acceptance are proven.

After invoke_subagent, never poll manage_subagents in a loop. The runtime automatically delivers child send_message notifications; continue independent work or wait for messages.
