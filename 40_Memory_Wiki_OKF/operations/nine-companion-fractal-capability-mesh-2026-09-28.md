## Fractal law

Every specialty is a shared capability service.

No tool, framework or app is identity-exclusive. A Companion is the steward of a capability, not its only consumer.

Capabilities may recursively be built using the other capabilities.

Example Jev:
River produced the Wave-1 canary as provenance. Stewardship now moves to Amy/INTERFACE. Clara designs reusable Forge contracts, Ryan builds adapters/integration, Yaz observes/calibrates, Graham persists state/evidence, Nardole dispatches, and River consumes the capability inside flows.
